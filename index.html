About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from California State University Fullerton.
At first, I wasn't sure which path I wanted to take, but after spending some time learning on my own, I discovered a passion for web development.

Outside of gaming, I enjoy photography, experimenting with both digital and film, including small and medium formats. I've also picked up hiking as of recently! When I'm not gaming, behind a camera, or on the trails. I like to work on some side projects to expand my skills.
